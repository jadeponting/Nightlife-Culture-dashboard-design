# The working record

*How each chapter was made. One file per chapter, `chNN-workings.md`, holding word for word the sections that stood in the chapter at draft 2 and were moved here on 14 October 2026 when the book was finished: "Before you start"; the symmetry test (what the chapter expected against what it found); "Open questions and flags"; "Looking back" (what the chapter did to the chapters before it); "Gaps found while writing this chapter"; the design system and Decision Book conflict log; and, for Chapter 16, its postscript. Chapter 0's file holds its gaps table.*

Nothing here is a rule or an open choice. The rules are in `06-register/rule-register.md`; the open choices, with the book's steer on each, in `00-plan/DECISIONS.md`; the probe candidates in `03-tests/PROBES.md`. The flags these sections carry (`[Further research]`, `[Prototype flag]`, `[Validate with users]`, `[Revisit → Ch n]`, `[Revisit at end]`, `[Rich reference]`, `[Counterbalance → X]`) are explained in `AGENTS.md`; the chapters no longer carry them. Candidates for the Decision Book and the design system are in each chapter's conflict log here, and in the decision log's group J.

Line numbers in these files are those of the draft-2 text, kept at `archive/04-chapters-draft2/`.
