# Working record · Chapter 12 — Recognising your place

*How this chapter was made. These sections stood in the chapter at draft 2 and were moved here, word for word, on 14 October 2026 when the book was finished. They are the record of the method: what the chapter expected against what it found, what it did to the chapters before it, the gaps it met, where it met the design system and the Decision Book, and the loose ends it flagged. Nothing here is a rule; the rules are in the register, the open choices in the decision log, the probes in `03-tests/PROBES.md`.*

## Before you start

This is the twelfth chapter of a research book written for Nightlife Culture, a creative policy studio led by Carly Heath, about how dashboards work and why. The book exists to support the Vibe Map — a planned public, non-commercial, interactive account of the people, activity, value and support systems that sustain a place's nightlife culture, built first for the West of England. You do not need to have read the earlier chapters. Where this one leans on them it says so and gives the finding in a line, and it argues from its own sources.

The chapter's conventions are the book's. Every finding is marked by **kind** — *Standard* (a published rule from a standards body, a regulator, a design system or a method text, with the sub-kind named), *Evidence* (a measured result, with what was measured and on whom), *Reading* (this book's interpretation of evidence or standards), *Practice* (what a named project does, read for method), or *Nightlife Culture* (the house's own position) — and by **tier**: *Fixed* (holds in any dashboard), *Adaptable* (holds unless a stated reason overrides it) or *Decorative* (open). "Fixed as a stance" means the house has decided it and the chapter gives the grounds; "Fixed as a procedure" means the rule is a thing to do, not a thing to believe. A dagger (†) marks a figure or quotation taken from an abstract, a secondary account or a press summary rather than from the paper's body; "secondary" marks a source read only through another; "(read once)" marks a quotation returned once by the fetch tool without a presence check; "reconstructed" marks a quotation assembled from fragments the tool returned separately. Sub-kinds of Standard used here and defined in earlier chapters: Standard (law), Standard (regulator), Standard (official statistics), Standard (design system), Standard (method), Standard (community). "Evidence of method" marks a methodological paper read for what it establishes about a method rather than for a measured result. Flags in square brackets — `[Further research]`, `[Prototype flag]`, `[Validate with users]`, `[Revisit → Ch n]`, `[Revisit at end]`, `[Rich reference]`, `[Counterbalance → Ch n]` — are defined in Chapter 0 and used here as there. Findings in the body carry their kind in italics at the end of a paragraph; tiers are given in the rules table.

A few of the book's working words recur, and this chapter adds some of its own. The *two columns* are version 1's name for the deck's slides 7 and 8: what systems already count about a place (incidents, licensing, complaints, footfall, spend) beside what a scene holds and almost nobody counts (belonging, trust, care, talent, joy) — the *counted* column and the *missed* column. A place's *identity* is its profile — what it is like, what it sounds like, the shape of its week, who comes — and its *conditions* are what it has or lacks: space, support, funding, affordability, security of tenure. The *comparability gate* is the house's eight-criterion check that two places' figures can be set side by side — concept and numerator/denominator; unit and price basis; reference period and vintage; geography and its basis; population or universe; classification system and version; observed against modelled method; treatment of uncertainty and suppression (Style Guide §12; Comparative Methodology v1.0). A *spread* is Chapter 7's display of every unit's value as a distribution with one unit marked and no positions given; an *ordered list* is a table or list of units in rank order with positions. The *poster rule* is the house's limit on a headline: five words where possible, ten at most; the *three-phones test* is Chapter 11's — a reader on a phone says the sentence, the figure it rests on and a next step; the *walk* is Chapter 10's friendly-intruder test of a view's filters against public lists; the *forum* is Chapter 10's standing body of people from the scenes, consulted on what is shown; the *safeguards record* and the *data contract* are Chapter 10's and Chapter 4's records beneath the data; a *provenance tier* is the house's text code for how a figure was obtained; an *address* is Chapter 11's canonical web address for a view; *endorsement* is Chapter 11's finding that whatever a page shows first is read as what the maker stands behind; *intensity* is Chapter 6's family of activity measures per head or per unit, which this chapter treats as neither identity nor condition but as counted activity. The *ELS* is the ONS's Explore Local Statistics service; *OA*, *LSOA* and *MSOA* are output areas and lower- and middle-layer super output areas, the census's small-area units; *MAUP* is the modifiable areal unit problem, section 3. The *unit* is the area a figure is for — an output area, a ward, a borough, a drawn neighbourhood — and the *scene scale* is version 1's phrase for the unit at which a scene lives, which is rarely an administrative one. A *rank* is an ordering of units published as the maker's ordering; a *match* is an ordering made for one reader from their own weights and shown to them alone. A *label* is the name given to a category, cluster or type of place. The *crop test* is version 1's rule that whatever is cut out of a view and carried away must still say what it is. The *held status* is Chapter 10's: a declared status for a category of thing that is known to the house and not shown, never a marker on a named room or business. The *three user groups* are the studio's testing groups for the prototype, named in its design repository and not here. *Form 696* is a piece of licensing history in London that Carly holds; it enters here as her frame, not as anything this book researched. The *deck* is the house's trusted-partner presentation; the *brief* is this book's governing brief, whose failure register this chapter answers. "Version 1" is the first draft of this book, written in August 2026 and now archived; its chapters are cited as positions to test, not as sources. And *recognise* is the chapter's own word, which section 1 splits into four senses and numbers, because the failure the brief names — "they do not recognise their place" — turns out to be several: four senses of the word in section 1, and five failures with tests in section 10.

A note on sources. Four research agents worked without web search this sprint (the shared budget was spent before they began), from direct addresses only, and several of the chapter's founding texts could not be reached at source: Lynch's *Image of the City*, Stevens's 1946 paper, the body of Coulton's 2001 study, Birt's member-checking procedure, Kennedy and Hill's paper on the feeling of numbers. Where that is so the chapter says so, quotes nothing from memory, and carries the thread as `[Further research]`. What was reached was checked string by string, and the verifier's pass is recorded in the log.

## 11 · The symmetry test

The chapter's positions, tested against the house's and version 1's as written before this research.

*Value first.* Version 1: Fixed, because repetition strengthens the opposing frame. **Holds as a stance, on different grounds**; the repetition reason is withdrawn and ARG-12 is logged as a conflict.

*The counted column never omitted.* **Holds**, and the asset tradition's own reviewers say why: assets "alongside needs", never instead.

*Equal visual dignity.* **Holds as a stance**; untested; LAY-08 is the ground; a prototype test is named.

*Gaps rendered full-size and labelled.* **Holds**; CALVI confirmed and narrowed; the Analysis Function's symbols supply the labels; "a silent gap reads as less value" is a reading, marked.

*No shared axis.* **Holds** as a standard and a reading, with its measured cost stated.

*The bridging sentence.* **Holds**, with no guidance behind it and the ONS's worded-comparison rule as the model.

*Identity never ranked.* **Holds as a stance**, corrected: not a fact about data; the house's question makes it nominal, and the measurement literature says why it can only be a stance; the data contract records the line.

*Conditions gated.* **Holds**, and is now a standard with official cousins (Principle 14; Code 3.0 6.6, 6.7, 7.4, 9.3; the ELS's coherence check).

*Comparison at the scene scale.* **Holds as a declared choice**, not a discovery: MAUP makes any unit's finding one finding among possible findings; the administrative unit is shown beside it.

*Default self-over-time plus chosen peers.* Chapter 7's; **holds**; the ONS and the Indices say the same.

*Incomparability rendered.* **Holds**, in the form that needs no new literacy; the Hasse diagram is a flag.

*No view survives cropping as a ranking.* **Holds as a stance**; the measured crop is wider than ranking and the remedy is provenance inside the frame.

*Discovery orderings personal, decomposed, on request.* **Holds**; the founding study's numbers corrected; the mechanism supplied (content cues help; social-proof cues inflate; personalisation hurts effectiveness).

*Explanations for effectiveness, not persuasion.* **Holds, strengthened.**

*Adjacency against narrowing.* **Holds as a probe**, with the measured caution on novelty.

*The money view as absence made visible.* **Holds**, with two additions: the official method is a composite with a judgement step, and a gap in grants data is a gap in publishing.

*Opt-in, granular, revocable discoverability.* **Restated under Chapter 10**: a category's status, consulted, governed and agreed; never a marker on a unit; self-listing is a precedent for a category's rule.

*Informal scenes default to aggregate character.* **Holds** as Chapter 10's held status applied to a category.

*The five-second tests; the losers' test; the Form 696 test.* **Hold**, the first sharpened by Chapter 11 (sentence, figure, next step) and the last run with Chapter 10's walk.

*"Robustness reads as reality."* **Half-confirmed**: granularity is read as confidence; the feared cost of showing uncertainty is not found.

*The house's.* "Lead with abundance" and "all vibes are valid" are stances and are kept as stances with the chapter's grounds; "Does it paint a recognisable picture?" is the member-check procedure and gains a record; the voice rule against defining places through failure is the label rule's test; the comparability gate is a quality dimension made a checklist; "a venue is not a scene" (Chapter 3) is Straw's definition applied.

*The studio's own place-page.* Which scale the Vibe Map lands on is a declared choice and is read as an endorsement, and the page says so. **A question the studio answers.**

## Design system (Style Guide v1.5) and Decision Book — conflict log

**Agrees.** The comparability flag (charts) is this chapter's gate on the face, and is now a quality dimension with official cousins. The voice rule against "Defining people or places only through failure or need" is the label rule's test. The slide test "Does it paint a recognisable picture? … Record who is missing and what contributors change" is the review procedure, now with a record. The recommendation-strength meter "as filled ordinal steps in ink, never a colour scale" is the same discipline this chapter applies to the two columns. The Decision Book's DAT-18, DAT-21 and TBL-17 are the crop rule's spine; LAY-08 is equal dignity's ground; the geography rules (boundary vintage; stated lookup) are the unit rule's.

**Conflicts.** One, direct. ARG-12, "Never repeat the opposing frame in order to deny it", tier Fixed, reason "Repetition reinforces it" (Decision Book Chapter 16): the reason no longer holds against the corrections literature, and the competitive-framing evidence says strength decides, not count. The chapter proposes the rule be re-based (value-first as a declared endorsement; no needless repetition) or retired; logged for the Decision Book, not changed here.

**Silent on.** A unit declaration on figures about places; a core-and-bands form for vague places; a reader-drawn unit; the identity/conditions split as a layout boundary and a data-contract field; a fixed-order profile component and a bar on filled radars; a label-writing rule and per-place hedge; in-frame provenance for charts (title, unit, source, base, gate, snapshot) against the design system's own preference for text outside images; matching and the display of weights; a review-and-errors footer; the recognition record. All logged for the design-system conversation.
