# The deviation log

*One file, in the repository, for every departure from a rule in the register. Chapter 16 §5 derived the entry and §11 states it once; Chapter 0's glossary points here. Three types of entry share the nine-line spine: a **departure** (the Decision Book's override log), an **expressive element** (Chapter 14's boundary log) and a **borrowed mechanism** (Chapter 15's precedent record). An entry is written before the departure ships, in the same change; for a departure from a rule tiered Fixed the build refuses the change without one (Chapter 16 §8). An accepted entry is never edited; it is superseded by a new one and kept. Entries are numbered sequentially and numbers are not reused.*

## The nine-line spine (every type)

| Line | What it holds | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Rule and tier | The register's identifier, the rule's wording, and its tier in the register's sense | A departure from a requirement, a sequence and a default are three different things |
| Trigger | What made the departure necessary, specific to this case — the data, the reader, the device, the deadline | A generic reason is what the regulator found in seventy of seventy-four; "lack of time or knowledge" is not one |
| Alternative | What was done instead, and the alternative that was considered and declined, with why | A departure with "a reasonable alternative" available is expedient by the coding standard's rule |
| Buys and costs | What the break buys, from the house's list — attention, retention, comprehension, context, register or fit — and what it costs, named | "An unnamed break is a mistake made confidently"; a purchase without a price is a bet |
| Test and result | The form — recomputation, record check, scan, removal, reader test, adversarial walk — and what it showed, as a result and not a tick | "it is not the act of ticking off a checklist … but performance of the actions it calls for" |
| Who decided, who checked | Two names; the second not the first, and where the departure changes how a place is shown, someone from the place | The regimes that work have a reader; the waivers nobody re-read were granted by those who wanted them |
| Date | When the entry was written, before the departure ships; and, for an entry written after, that it was | A record born "accepted" records a conclusion; the quality vocabulary keeps two words for before and after |
| Review | The date by which the entry is re-read, or the condition on which it lapses, and who owns it | Near-universal in the regimes read; absent in the house's four lines; the build lists the overdue |
| Status | Proposed; accepted; superseded by; lapsed — with the old entry kept and never edited | "If a decision is reversed, we will keep the old one around, but mark it as superseded." |

*The trigger line's "seventy of seventy-four" is the chapter's reading of the FRC's 2021 finding that four of seventy-four explanations were high quality; the regulator did not say the other seventy were generic.*

## The expressive type — Chapter 14's lines, added to the spine

An entry of this type is written for every expressive element before it ships; the build's removal check fails where the entry is missing (Chapter 16 §8). The spine's *rule and tier* line carries the tier claimed for the element; its *buys and costs* line carries the purchase in Chapter 14's template; its *test and result* line carries the four removals and the fifth check. The lines below are Chapter 14's own, as printed in its boundary log.

| Line | What it holds | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Element | What the expressive element is, where it appears, and the version of the display it was added to | A register entry outlives the person who made the choice |
| Tier claimed | Decorative; specific sensory experience; or a named Adaptable choice at the encoding level | The tier is the argument's first move (Decision Book Ch 18) |
| Purchase | What it buys, in the affective-intent template — "The viewer will [observe / position / strengthen / connect / behave] [noun]" — limited to what expression is measured to buy | An unnamed break is a mistake made confidently |
| Touches | Which view, encoding or claim it sits on or near | Palette moves feeling more than data; the entry must say what it is next to |
| Mono | What the greyscale render lost or changed | Hue is the commonest leak |
| Silent | What muting lost; for sound-as-content, where the text document is | WCAG 1.2.1; 1.4.2 |
| Still | What disabling motion lost; where the pause control is | ONS; WCAG 2.2.2 (Chapter 13) |
| Undressed | What stripping the register lost or changed | Mackinlay's second clause |
| Stripped beside dressed | Whether the two versions say the same thing — no added size, order, brightness or cause — and, for a form, its estimated glance cost against a bar | The fifth check |
| Twin and identification | Where the precise twin is, how many steps away, and the words that identify the element to a reader who cannot see or hear it | Ottley; WCAG's "descriptive identification" for a sensory experience |
| Tested, and status | Whether readers saw the stripped and dressed versions and what each took from the data — figures and trend agreeing is the pass | Chapter 14 §11 |

## The borrowed type — Chapter 15's lines, added to the spine

An entry of this type is written for every mechanism the build considers borrowing from another sector — adopted, adapted or declined — before it ships. The lines are Chapter 15's precedent record, as printed.

| Line | What it holds | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Mechanism | What is borrowed, in one sentence, and the section of the record it feeds | A borrowed rule lives in the record it serves, not in this one |
| Source | The project or body, the document, its version and date, and the date it was read | Rules change under the same name |
| Rule as printed | The threshold, interval, colour, cadence or wording, quoted, from one named document | A paraphrased rule drifts |
| Reason given | The source's own stated reason | The reason decides whether the rule transfers |
| Evidence | What was measured about it, by whom, on whom, with n; or "none found" | With a reason but no evidence or named cost, the entry is a bet, and says so |
| Condition or cost | What the mechanism needs, and what the display does while the need is unmet | A rule adopted without its condition fails silently |
| What the house changes | Any departure from the printed rule, and why | The departure is the house's, and is owned |
| Status | Adopted; adapted; declined, with the reason; or awaiting a condition | The record is a decision list |
| Review | When the source document is to be re-read for change, and who owns the entry | Sources retire |
