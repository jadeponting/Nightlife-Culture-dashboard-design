# Dashboard Design — the book on one page

*Fourteen chapters, one decision each. Poster line first; the sentence underneath does the work. 18 August 2026.*

---

**1 · The default view — THE LANDING VIEW IS THE CLAIM.**
Defaults are read as endorsements (measured), so the opening screen must speak the instrument's sentence in five seconds, cold, with nothing crucial behind a click.

**2 · The two columns — WHAT'S MISSED STANDS BESIDE WHAT'S MEASURED.**
Value and economics get equal dignity, never a shared axis — and the gaps in the value column *are* the argument, not a weakness to hide.

**3 · The map — A MAP IS A THINKING TOOL, NOT A POSTCODE PLOT.**
Say what a mark is and what the denominator is before drawing anything; aggregates swamp the grassroots (78% of events, 32% of revenue), so the small scene reads *uncounted*, never *absent*.

**4 · The felt layer — YOU CAN'T SCRAPE A VIBE.**
Feeling is co-produced, so it is collected with the people it belongs to — and every contribution carries "what this changed," so giving data visibly does something.

**5 · Comparison without a league table — IDENTITY IS NEVER RANKED.**
Conditions can be compared, always gated and carrying uncertainty; identity never can. The crop test enforces it: no screenshot of any state yields a league table.

**6 · The measure on screen — THE RECIPE ON THE FACE.**
Every composed number recomputes from parts shown beside it, holds a claim ladder for what nightlife originated, and stays open to revision as a living series.

**7 · Discovery — FIND YOUR TRIBE, NOT A TOP TEN.**
Matching, not ranking; scenes consent to being findable and can withdraw; the money view makes underinvestment visible; and it survives the Form 696 test — enforcement gains nothing unchosen.

**8 · Gaps, uncertainty, freshness — THE INSTRUMENT AGES VISIBLY.**
Every gap carries its reason, every state its heartbeat; stale must look stale, and retirement is designed, not silent. Tested by walking to the worst state with bad intent.

**9 · What an interaction must earn — MOST PEOPLE NEVER CLICK.**
10–15% touch controls, so static reading always works and every interaction names its purchase — and the "guess before you see" moment doubles as perception data.

**10 · Story and exploration — THE POSTER BESIDE THE DOOR.**
A five-word opening, then free exploration (stories don't beat it — measured); each place gets a templated story in house voice, and the instrument publishes how it argues.

**11 · Accessible as the good version — THE PLACE PLAYS FOR EVERYONE.**
Audio is the product *and* the access route, converged not bolted on; the written spine is the guaranteed path; the blind fan reaches the sighted reader's answer.

**12 · The gallery — NOBODY HAS BUILT THIS.**
Fifteen exemplars and incumbents, each priced (what rule it breaks, why it holds) — and confirmed white space: no cultural-value, origination or consented-scene display exists anywhere.

**13 · Make it look like art — THE CLAIM PICKS THE GENRE.**
This instrument's genre is nightlife's own visual tradition — the club poster, not the dashboard; and strong evidence must never be made to look weaker by timid design.

**14 · Breaking these rules well — DEVIATION IS EARNED BY TEST AND RECORD.**
Thirteen removal tests, ~150 seeded rules, a four-line override log — and nine places this instrument would be the first of its kind. If half survive testing, it's not a better dashboard; it's a new kind of thing.

*Every line above carries its evidence in its chapter; nothing here is new. The book is drafted; nothing in it is finished until it has survived you.*
