## The decision

**What does the strongest work in the field actually do — and what does each piece's success or failure transfer to this instrument?**

---

## The 60-second version

> Twelve exemplars and three incumbents, each read as technique rather than admired. The
> pattern across the strong work: **the message is carried in words and structure; the craft
> is spent on making one authored thing land; interaction is optional everywhere it exists.**
> The pattern across the failures: containers outliving contents, results gated behind forms,
> instruments that ran at a platform's pleasure.
>
> And one meta-finding: almost nothing here was formally evaluated. The gallery is a museum
> of confident practice, not a body of evidence — which is why this book's probes matter:
> the instrument being built from these chapters would be among the first of its kind to
> **measure itself.**

## A · The storytellers

**1. "This is a teenager" — The Pudding (Alvin Chang, 2024).** *[Audited live, 16 Aug 2026.]*
Exemplar-first opening ("We'll call him Alex. It's 1997…") over a clickable field of real
longitudinal lives. **Exemplifies:** Chapter 1's exemplar-first pattern at its best — meaning
at scroll-zero; dispersion shown within groups, which is the hardest honest thing an
aggregate display can do. **Breaks:** provenance-on-face (date in footer). **Why it holds:**
the words carry the story; the field of marks carries the population; each redeems the
other's weakness. **Transfer:** the felt layer's one-mark-per-person forms; the authored
exemplar as door.

**2. "Is the Love Song Dying?" — The Pudding (2024).** *[From raw sweep; not re-fetched.]*
The reader removes categories according to their own definition of a love song, and the
conclusion changes. **Exemplifies:** definition-as-interaction — the contested term *is* the
control. **Transfer:** directly reusable for "what counts as nightlife": let the sceptic
exclude categories and watch the value figure hold (or move) — the definitional argument made
explorable instead of asserted. Candidate probe.

**3. "I Want a Better Catastrophe" flowchart (Boyd, Dörk et al.).** *[From raw sweep.]* Audio
narration plus progressive disclosure; the rare interactive with a **companion peer-reviewed
paper and released evaluation data**. **Exemplifies:** the evaluated exception — proof that
publishing your instrument's evaluation is possible and distinguished. **Transfer:** the
standard this book's probes should meet.

**4. The ONS 331 local stories.** *[Ledger, Ch 1.]* Semi-automated written narrative at
national scale. **Exemplifies:** Chapter 10's spine — story per place without a scrolly in
sight. **Transfer:** already claimed as the house pattern.

## B · The instruments

**5. Our World in Data / Grapher.** *[Audited live, 16 Aug 2026.]* Provenance and relative
dates as homepage furniture; open-source stack; defaults to World-plus-continents (an
exemplar aggregation, never a random country); every chart's config inspectable.
**Exemplifies:** the inspectable instrument — credibility through open workings; Chapter 6's
recipe-on-face at platform scale. **Breaks:** noun-phrase titles (against the ten-word message
rule). **Transfer:** open the workings; write better titles than they do.

**6. ONS Explore Local Statistics.** *[Audited live.]* Input-first entry; similar-authority
clusters; spread displays; an official-statistics service that won a Royal Statistical
Society award with user research behind it. **Exemplifies:** Chapter 5's
comparison-as-context, institutionally proven. **Breaks:** the empty entry state proves
nothing before the postcode (Chapter 1's critique). **Transfer:** clusters and spreads yes;
blank front door no.

**7. OECD Better Life Index.** *[Audited live; history from raw.]* The reweightable flower —
and a decade later, its deepest finding is the back-channel: tens of thousands of shared
weightings as collected data. Its current landing has decayed to brochure-and-downloads.
**Exemplifies:** reader-weighting as collection (Chapters 7, 10). **Warns:** message-first
decays into brochure-first when the exemplar leaves the front door. **Transfer:** the
back-channel; and never let the flower retreat behind a PDF.

**8. Every Noise at Once.** *[From Ch 7 sweep.]* Position-as-sound, equal clickability,
places that play — frozen the day its maker left the platform. **Exemplifies and warns:** the
finest similarity surface and the platform-dependency lesson in one artefact. **Transfer:**
Chapter 7 took both.

**9. Gapminder.** *[Tools page unfetchable — inference only, marked in the audit.]* The
animated scatter as public argument; a quiz as front door ("You are probably wrong about…").
**Exemplifies:** prediction-before-reveal at brand scale — their entire identity is Chapter
9's guess-moment. **Transfer:** the misconception quiz is a proven engagement door for
evidence with a perception gap — precisely this instrument's situation (the public's guess at
the night's worth versus the measured figure).

## C · The neighbours

**10. Fragapane, "Noise Pollution" (MoMA collection).** *[From raw sweep.]* Expressive
encoding of environmental sound data, museum-collected. **Exemplifies:** Chapter 13's
institutional proof that art-register data work can be canonical — on a subject adjacent to
this one. **Transfer:** the licence, with the Decision Book's condition (precise version
alongside) intact.

**11. The BBC postcode interactives.** *[Audited live, via mirror.]* Question-in-your-voice
headline; four paragraphs of stakes; then the input; national story until you localise.
**Exemplifies:** message-then-input — the pattern Chapter 1's worked example chose.
**Transfer:** already taken.

**12. Doughnut Economics (as display).** *[House reference frame.]* One figure carrying a
whole economic argument; non-compensatory by shape. **Exemplifies:** Chapter 6's boundary
form. **Transfer:** the nightlife doughnut probe.

## D · The incumbents, read for display only

**13. Thriving Places Index.** *[Audited live, 16 Aug 2026.]* Select-first Power BI embed —
a dropdown before any meaning; documented 0–10 colour semantics against a national average;
methodology and indicator list published and dated. **Exemplifies:** publishing your
methodology (genuinely good practice this book keeps). **Warns:** the instrument-panel front
door serves only the professional who already knows their authority (Chapter 1); the
against-average colour scale makes half of England "low" by construction (Chapter 5's
spread-not-average critique). **Transfer:** publish like them; open like the BBC.

**14. Creative Footprint.** *[Audited live; arithmetic from raw.]* Mission-verbs landing;
results gated behind a form; a headline score not reproducible from its published parts.
**Warns:** three of this book's Fixed rules broken at once — no meaning at the front door, the
gated result, the undisclosed recipe. **Transfer:** the counter-example the recipe-on-face
rule cites by name.

**15. The London Night Time Data Observatory.** *[Verified on live pages, 16 Aug 2026.]* The
links-hub whose contents aged while its container smiled; one dataset in ~24 current — the
externally-automated one. **Warns:** Chapters 1 and 8's founding case. **Transfer:** the
heartbeat, the per-layer refresh mechanism, the visible retirement.
