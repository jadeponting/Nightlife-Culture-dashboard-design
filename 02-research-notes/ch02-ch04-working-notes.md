directly and from evidence already gathered and verified in earlier phases. This note records

## Chapter 2 — The two columns

- **Decision Book Ch 16** (read directly): the Othering & Belonging framing evidence —
  "invulnerable to facts that do not fit within a narrative"; never repeat the opposing frame to
  deny it; lead with the shared value; the opposing-narrative reader test. These supplied step

- **Style Guide §06/§12** (read directly): the evidence standard; provenance chips; the
  ink-steps rule barring moral colour ramps.

- **Do No Harm** (agent-fetched earlier): reference categories, ordering, deficit framing.
- **CALVI** (agent-fetched earlier): 0.39 baseline; missing data and normalisation hardest to
  detect — the basis for rendering gaps at full size.
- **Raw notes** (unverified): the aggregation/commensurability literature informed the *shape*
  of the no-shared-axis rule; the rule stands on its own display argument rather than on those
  notes.

## Chapter 4 — The felt layer

**Sources, and how each was reached:**

- **"On the essence of vibe"** (Obsidian, staged and read directly 16 Aug): the co-production
  theory — "You can't consume a vibe… It needs you there, to be part of it, for it to exist at
  all"; the arena critique ("you are chattel"; "the vibe is only as good as the one you brought
  with you"); "creating vibes is a science"; music as conversation (the MC redirecting the set);
  belonging cues at street level (bar staff remembering the order; "you ok to get home?"; "I
  came alone, I left with friends"). This is the chapter's spine and the source of the
  co-production display rule.
- **Vibe Setting Framework** (read directly, pp 1–20, 25–33, 43–51 — **confidential**):
  - Workshop 2: the extraction finding; the data-ethics questions; "what consent processes are
    needed, especially for informal or underground networks that may not want visibility?"
  - Workshop 4: belonging as "more than presence… feeling recognised, safe enough, useful,
    welcome and able to contribute without having to become someone else"; the small-cues list
    (who is visible, who is promoted, whose genre is taken seriously…); the three-statement
    spectrum-line exercise (the human dot plot); 125 post-its → 10 design principles; principle
    3 (visible, evidenced listening — "the CBS needs to show its workings") → the "what this
    changed" provenance field; principle 9 (speak the language of the scene); "governance is
    not just a legal requirement. It is a cultural practice."
- **From the raw vibe-strand research** (agent-gathered earlier; the load-bearing items are in
  the sources ledger): Böhme (atmospheres are made) and Michels (the three-layer empirical
  programme) for the produced register; ISO 12913-1/-3 for the perceived register (the
  circumplex including *vibrant*; medians/IQRs; **note: sample/draft pages, purchase before
  publication-grade citation**); Anderson and Garcia-Mispireta for the excess register; Fetters,
  Curry & Creswell for discordance-as-finding; Reed et al. 2025 for participatory mapping at
  n=12 and the safety paradox; Queering the Map for brigading-before-moderation.
- **Decision Book Ch 7/8/18** (read directly): the expressive licence (precise version ships
  alongside); disclosure at unit level; meaning-in-the-decorative-layer as the failure mode for
  quotes-as-wallpaper.

**Steer additions (17 Aug):** the digital human dot plot as collection instrument ("what does
your city sound like" as playable survey); the studio advocacy model (city study + audience
