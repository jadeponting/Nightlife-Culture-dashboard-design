The interactive evidence instrument: eighteen decisions

A research design, not a product. Each card is one question a builder is forced to answer. The order is the order in which the decisions resolve — an upstream error cascades and cannot be recovered downstream.

Nightlife Culture · version 0.1 · 16 August 2026 · Derived from six desk-research strands. Canonical copy: ncvi-research/RESEARCH-DESIGN-v0.1.md

The resolution stack

When two considerations compete, the higher one wins. Rung 1 is not rung 3: a number can be methodologically impeccable and still expose someone.

  1. Exposure and consent — who is harmed by being counted
  2. Referential integrity — can the claim be traced to a thing and a source
  3. Inferential integrity — does the method support the claim
  4. The decision it enters — what the number has to be true enough for
  5. Legibility — can the reader read it correctly
  6. Standing — will anyone else reconcile to it
  7. Register — how it feels, what it looks like

Reading the tier meter. Filled steps out of five show how much of the decision the research already settles — five filled means a right answer exists and is measurable; one filled means the literature offers a vocabulary and no verdict. The level is written alongside, so the meter carries nothing on its own.

Ordinal quantities are encoded by amount filled in one ink, never as a colour scale, because a red–amber–green ramp cannot contrast against the ground and against itself simultaneously and fails worst under protanopia. Measured on this sheet: filled to unfilled 4.47:1, unfilled to card 3.61:1, body text to card 16.12:1, muted text to card 6.49:1.

Part 0 · The spine

Chapter 1

Whose decision does this have to survive, and what does that decision need to be true?

Mixed — Fixed that one instrument serves one decision; Adaptable which
What the research settles
Green Book Review 2025: a single BCR "is not enough," no arbitrary thresholds, BCR under one may still be value for money. But unmonetised benefits live in an annex and monetised ones live in the ratio. Munzner: abstraction error has no immediate validation and cascades.
Refusal test
Name the decision in writing before any encoding work. CHC's Evidence Bank covers museums, galleries, theatres, churches, cinema — no nightclub, no grassroots venue.

Chapter 2

Who is exposed by being counted, and what would tell you before it happens?

Mostly Fixed — mechanisms documented, thresholds measurable
What the research settles
Form 696: private addresses, music style, target audience, originally ethnic group, 21 London boroughs, 2008–2017. Strava: voluntary traces exposed military bases. Queering the Map: brigaded offline in 2018, moderation built afterwards.
The finding that cuts the other way
Arts largely follow gentrification; displacement unobservable in the two strongest studies. The honest risk is vocabulary and coordinates, not causation.

Part 1 · Referential integrity

Chapter 3

What is the unit, and what survives when it changes?

Adaptable — but Fixed that it is declared before minting
What the research settles
Every precedent picks one unit and inherits its blindness. Straw: a scene is defined by differentiation, not membership — so "I'm part of this scene" is not a claim like "I live here." Bowker & Star's torque: venue biography versus register, not retrofittable.
Refusal test
A venue closes and reopens, new name, new operator, same building. State what persists, before the first ID is minted.

Chapter 4

Does the identifier have to be adopted by others, and what do you owe if it is?

Mostly Fixed — the adoption mechanism is documented
What the research settles
ROR grew 356% annually against 12% for the scheme it replaced. UPRN was blocked by licensing, not technology. ORCID's trick is collecting the iD via API, never typed. MusicBrainz owes merge-with-redirect forever.
The opening
There appears to be no live-music venue identifier standard. Ticket Atlas already mints stable IDs never derived from a platform slug — the architecture is right.

Chapter 5

Is the definitions atlas a dictionary or a crosswalk?

Mostly Fixed — boundary-object theory is settled
What the research settles
"Consensus is not necessary for cooperation." Standardise how, not why — procedural consensus displaces theoretical conflict. MVT's Grassroots Music Venue definition got adopted by ACE and DCMS with an Elephant Test in it. Vagueness was not a bug.
Refusal test
Distribute residual categories, never concentrate them. A single top-level "Other" is where queer, unlicensed and informal spaces go to become invisible to policy.

Chapter 6

Where did this number come from, and can a machine answer that?

Mostly Fixed — PROV-O and RDA give an implementable answer
What the research settles
RDA Dynamic Data Citation: version and never delete, run a query store, checksum the result, timestamp the query, and assign the identifier to the query, not a copy of the data. Reported effort: a few person-months over six to twelve.
The finding that decides whether it works
Formal metadata gets bypassed because contributors describe data "for invisible, unknown future users… from whom they receive little if any benefit." Metadata process, not only product.

Part 2 · Inferential integrity

Chapter 7

What does your aggregation rule commit you to, and can you state it in real units?

Fixed — measurable, and there is a right answer to compute
What the research settles
Under additive aggregation (21,1,1,1) equals (6,6,6,6). Translated: a corporate late-night drinking economy with a collapsed grassroots scene scores the same as a modest broad ecology. The 2010 HDI valued a year of life at $0.51 in Zimbabwe and ~$9,000 in the richest countries.
Refusal test — the best one in the research
How many new venues does your index say compensate for the loss of one forty-year-old grassroots institution? If you cannot state the number, you do not understand your instrument.

Chapter 8

Do your weights mean what you say they mean?

Fixed — the sensitivity index is computable and publishable
What the research settles
Asking a panel for importance then using a weighted sum is internally inconsistent. HDI weights were "little more than cosmetic." THES: teaching quality 20% nominal weight, 21% of variance; peer review 40% nominal, 81%. Budget allocation degrades past 10–12 items.
The incumbent fails this
Creative Footprint Rotterdam publishes 8.58 / 5.90 / 5.41 and a headline of 6.10. The mean of its own parts is 6.63. No weights disclosed anywhere.

Chapter 9

Can the instrument be audited, and at what N does the audit become impossible?

Fixed — published numeric thresholds, pass or fail
What the research settles
Indicator-to-sub-dimension r ≥ 0.3; Cronbach's α ≥ 0.70; one eigenvalue > 1.0 per dimension; 5,000 Monte Carlo runs; publish a 90% confidence interval on rank, and treat wider than ten positions as unreliable. The EU Monitor concedes one city in five is a modelling artefact.
The decisive finding
This apparatus needs N in the hundreds. A scored index at N=2 is unauditable in principle. Not a preference about form.

Chapter 10

What comparative claims does your sample actually license?

Mostly Fixed — the method that fits is identifiable
What the research settles
QCA at 9–12 cases per place is at or below its floor: variants "systematically supplement empirical data with artificial data" and can "commit causal fallacies of severe magnitude." fsQCA relocates the arbitrariness to calibration.
What to do instead
Process tracing — straw-in-the-wind, hoop, smoking gun, doubly decisive. And Lijphart: with two places you can only reduce variables. Nine dimensions across two places is not a comparison; it is nine anecdotes.

Chapter 11

What can be published at every filter combination, not just the default view?

Fixed — a transplantable rule set already exists
What the research settles
ONS Census 2021 flexible table builder: marginal minimum, marginal dominance (≥20 outside the most common category), sparsity floors, variable limits, and classification completeness — users cannot request category subsets, which is what prevents differencing. Cell keys perturb consistently across datasets.
The compromise you may inherit if you declare it
Queries assessed independently, with no cumulative-risk tracking across a user's history. ONS accepts this for the census. Unstated, the same choice reads as ignorance.

Part 3 · The felt layer

Chapter 12

How first-class is the vibe, and what makes it evidence rather than decoration?

Two thirds measurable, one third representable only
What the research settles
Split it in three. Produced condition — fully researchable, and the layer where the resident DJ and door person become visible as skilled labour. Perceived quality — ISO 12913-3 gives an eight-attribute circumplex in which vibrant is a standardised perceptual attribute of a place. Singular excess — representable, not measurable.
The one decision that separates evidence from advocacy
Pre-commit to reporting discordance between the qualitative and quantitative strands as a finding rather than a failure.

Part 4 · Legibility

Chapter 13

Does the interaction carry meaning, and what evidence would settle it?

The default is settled; the exceptions are narrow and named
What the research settles
Adding narrative to exploratory visualisation is null to negative across ~4,300 sessions: the no-story group explored twice as long, 108.8s against 54s. Only 35.4% followed the intended sequence. The famous 10–15% click figure is conference lore, not a study.
The narrow exceptions
Prediction-before-reveal (~4pp, visual modality only, unfamiliar data only) and staged animated transitions (helps until over-staged). Everything else must beat a known null.

Chapter 14

How do you show uncertainty without making the number look worthless?

Fixed — the most empirically settled area in the research
What the research settles
Quantile dotplots at 50 dots reached 97% of optimal expected payoff (n=408), beating a no-uncertainty control and halving variance. Textual intervals were unstable. Hard visual boundaries induce inside/outside categorisation that "persists even with extensive instructions."
The reader-side finding that changes the interface
Baseline correctness on misleading charts is 0.39 against 0.80 on normal ones, and the hardest thing to detect is missing normalisation. Put the denominator in the interface, not the methods note.

Chapter 15

Who can use it, and is that a build step or a bespoke cost?

Fixed — auditable, and there is tooling
What the research settles
Chartability: 50 heuristics, 10 flagged Critical, 2–8 hours per audit, adopted by Microsoft, Highcharts, Jupyter and Vega-Lite. Olli converts Vega and Observable Plot specs into keyboard-navigable described trees, grounded in a 13-participant study.
The argument to make internally
Accessibility regulations almost certainly do not bind you. WCAG 2.2 AA is a distribution decision: a government user cannot embed a non-compliant product without inheriting the problem.

Part 5 · Standing

Chapter 16

What does your own standard already assert, and what would an external one add?

Reframed on review — a choice between two working models, not a ladder
What the research settles
Style Guide v1.4 §06 already declares its own precedence rule and contains refusals: "a comparison without this note is not publishable." The Code refuses nothing. Adopting it does not raise your floor — it makes your floor legible to people who will never read your style guide.
The case that reframes it
Centre for Thriving Places publishes the TPI, self-funded, and is not on the voluntary register. Carnegie UK, its nearest peer, is. Both work. The two things an internal standard cannot self-supply are independence and legibility to a stranger.

Chapter 17

What makes others adopt it, and what does success cost you?

Mostly Fixed — the mechanism repeats across five cases
What the research settles
Solve an acute visible failure; launch minimal; make the data un-encloseable; get one actor to require it; inherit an installed base; charge for services, not data; govern in public. FAIR's principle A2 — metadata accessible even when data is not — is the technical shape of "knowledge layer shared, data layer protected."
The live proof, in Bristol
Centre for Thriving Places gives away "headline scores and interactive maps" and funds the framework by tiered subscription — partly to ensure "investment comes from sources that will not seek to adapt its purpose or reach." A governance argument in a pricing statement.
The uncomfortable finding
Successful infrastructure becomes invisible. And you cannot have comparability across time and accuracy in the present. Pick, and say which.

Chapter 18

Advocacy or evidence: what happens when the number is inconvenient?

One Fixed mechanism; the rest is a position you take
What the research settles
A Culture Secretary admitted deploying contested impact statistics as "ammunition." Matarasso's study had no baseline, no control, ~75% non-response. An organisation trade bodies reconcile to is structurally exposed, because trade bodies are advocacy organisations.
The one mechanism that distinguishes you
A pre-registered methodology and a published correction policy, in place before you publish a number. DCMS has already conceded the sector's evaluation is incomparable — an external standard is welcome, not resented.

What this does not do

It does not choose the artefact, the robustness standard, or what "source of truth" means operationally. Those were asked as questions and correctly refused — they are what the research is for. It contains no rules: rules come from real artefacts breaking.
