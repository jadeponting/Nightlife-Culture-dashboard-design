# dashboard-design

Researching the methodology for an interactive evidence instrument — a nightlife cultural value
index or vibe map expressed interactively, at the frontier of design practice and defensible under
institutional and academic scrutiny.

**Nightlife Culture · started 16 August 2026**

---

## What this repository is

**Research, not a build.** The object of study is the methodology itself: what is known about
making this class of artefact well. The artefact comes later, out of the research.

This closes a known gap. Nightlife Culture Style Guide v1.4, section 15, gap 4:

> "**Dashboards.** No dashboard system exists yet, and one should not be built casually."

## Status

`00-plan/RESEARCH-PLAN-v0.3.md` — plan for approval. **No research conducted yet.**

## How it works

One chapter per sprint, in order, each building on the last. Draft, send, steer, continue. The
chapter template, the tier model and the gap register are carried from *The Decision Book*
(`~/Documents/GitHub/decision-book`, frozen) and cited rather than re-derived.

Two things govern what may be assumed:

- **§2a Axioms** — decisions already made. Cited, never re-argued.
- **§2b Candidate inventory** — existing datasets, methodologies and indices whose fitness for this
  purpose is a research question. Nothing here is a foundation until a chapter establishes that it is.
